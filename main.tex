  \section*{Preface}
  Thank you for opening up this book. This is a book dedicated to you and those who want to learn calculus from calculus I to IV. I, myself, have taken a very windy path down the road of learning calculus, and I hope that you reading the book would not have to waste precious time like how I did. While the book assumes no calculus or precalculus knowledge, it does require knowing algebra, trigonometry and basic set theory. Also, you may feel that the precalculus section's pace is very fast, and it is intentional, this is not a precalculus book. There are many great precalculus material out there and I encourage you reading them to gain a deeper understanding on certain topics. With that said, I feel that the first section should feel manageable to most of you. In addition, while this book will cover both the theory and application of calculus, it would be more computation based instead of being very rigorous. This is not an analysis book. Hence, some parts make lack mathematical rigour but I will attach good resources to all parts of the book if I believe some other source explains certain things better than I do. Lastly, read the book thoughtfully, do not just blindly flip through the pages to not waste your precious time. I wish all readers find joy and learn something in reading the book.
