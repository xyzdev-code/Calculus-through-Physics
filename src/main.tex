  \chapter*{Preface I: Calculus, the language of the universe}


  \scshape Calculus\normalfont, is the mathematical study of continuous change, and is arguably one of the most important tools in every physicist's toolkit. It is not just a bunch of methods to compute fancy looking equations (even though many students treat it as such). I wish that through reading the book, the reader will understand that calculus is the language that we speak to describe change in the universe in a concrete and rigorous way.
